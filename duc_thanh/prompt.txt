We are a group of 6 people belong to 4 different majors (Data Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineer, AI Engineer).

Our current project is SynthGuard: A synthetic-data augmentation platform for rare-attack IDS training whose privacy treatment is risk-differentiated rather than uniform. Instead of a single privacy audit at the end, every generated record is scored with a per-record membershipinference (MIA) confidence against the generator’s own training set; the synthetic corpus is then partitioned by that score — high-risk records are regenerated or filtered out, low-risk records are released. The resulting fidelity–utility–privacy frontier is compared against uniform baselines (uniform noise, uniform subsampling, uniform differential-privacy budget) at matched release sizes, making the field’s implicit assumption “synthetic = private” the actual object of study rather than a side note. A fixed holdout protocol ensures the generator and any shadow models never see the MIA evaluation split, without which privacy-leakage numbers would be uninterpretable. CTGAN/TVAE generation, the downstream real-vs-augmented IDS comparison, and the fidelity–utility–privacy scorecard tool remain the delivery vehicle.
